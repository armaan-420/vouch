### Participation and optional signup

- Participants finish product cards and optional nominations, then see the standings. Results remain available without an account.
- The bottom signup invitation is removed. A small Save my picks modal appears for signed-out participants who answered every selected card after they scroll past the third ranked product. On smaller boards it waits until the final product is viewed and the person scrolls.
- The modal appears once per experiment per tab visit, stays dismissed on reload, and restores the same scroll position. Keep exploring, Close, Escape and the backdrop dismiss it.
- The invitation says “Good taste deserves good company” and “See what people whose taste you trust recommend,” previews up to three of the participant’s opinions, and offers inline email-code signup.
- Product history is available now. Suggestions, recognition as a trusted contributor and member discounts are explicitly upcoming; following, feeds and advanced reputation remain outside this release.
- Verification continues into an explicit ownership/visibility review. Copy now says Save my picks and Your picks are saved. New profiles and opinions are private.

### Accounts, opinions and credibility

- Email codes expire after ten minutes, permit five attempts and are single-use. Resend throttling and source/email rate limits apply.
- Sessions use opaque HttpOnly cookies, Secure on HTTPS, with seven-day idle and thirty-day absolute expiry. Logout revokes the current session; expiry/account switching rotates owned browser identities before subsequent reads and writes.
- Claim tickets are browser-bound, cover an exact reviewed snapshot, expire after thirty minutes, and support idempotent retries. Existing experiment responses are preserved; claiming does not cast another vote.
- Profiles separate item vouches, passes and brand trust. One current opinion per account/product coexists with historical opinion snapshots; later votes can update the current collection.
- Public profiles and product pages show only opinions with both a public profile and public opinion. Known products work with zero public contributors.
- A pass does not prove firsthand use; brand trust is not item experience. Email control does not verify purchase, expertise or Wharton affiliation. First-picks recognition rewards passes and vouches equally.
- Account/auth records use isolated optimistic D1 JSON records. No destructive migration, vote reset, production backfill or import of local test data was needed.

### Standings and nomination refinements

- The top-right action becomes Create Account or My Account according to participant login.
- Active scheduled experiments show title | time remaining in the top bar. Unscheduled, upcoming, ended or manually closed experiments show no countdown; the standalone schedule block and expandable score guide are removed from standings. Formula details remain in Prototype info and source documentation.
- Brand, product and the participant’s action align left. Images, brands and product names open their contributor page; the separate See who vouched links are removed.
- Score colors: green at 8.0+, black 6.5–7.9, yellow 5.0–6.4, red below 5.0. Unrated stays neutral. Scoring mathematics is unchanged.
- Earlier releases since version 11 also simplified nominations: clear search text after selecting, inline removable chips, manual-name review, fixed See the board progression and a quiet in-field save checkmark. Source attribution remains in Prototype info.

### Checks and material limits

- 48 automated tests passed, covering authentication/session controls, ownership claims, concurrent claims, privacy, changed opinions, experiment history, scoring and existing experiment behavior.
- The local Cloudflare/D1 end-to-end smoke passed. Browser checks covered deferred prompting, incomplete/signed-in suppression, dismissal/position preservation, inline signup, wrong-code recovery, private saving and 390px layouts without horizontal overflow.
- Release production build and frontend syntax checks passed. Public HTTPS checks returned 200 for standings, account and product pages; deployed app.js, picks-prompt.js, accounts.js and style.css exactly match the release source. Anonymous organizer access remains 401.
- Production email auth reports available=true and previewDelivery=false. The verified Resend domain and restricted sending key are configured in server-only runtime settings. Actual production inbox delivery and full email sign-in have not been tested; the owner deferred that check.
- Anonymous experiment scores still deduplicate browsers, not accounts. Public contributor lists deduplicate accounts and are a separate population from anonymous scores.
- Pilot account/auth storage requires normalization, indexing and load/abuse testing before broad scale. Universal product-alias resolution, moderation, account deletion/export interfaces, advanced reputation, suggestions and discount redemption are not implemented.
- Signup conversion events and a conversion dashboard are not yet instrumented. The modal’s appearance is not evidence of signup lift, retention, expertise or improved purchase outcomes.
