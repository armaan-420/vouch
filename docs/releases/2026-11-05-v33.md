### Search and nominations

- The Board nomination form, post-voting optional nomination step, and organizer experiment product picker share one product-picker interaction. The search field stays full width; selected products appear in their own area below the results.
- Selecting a catalog result preserves the query and results. Full rows are selectable with visible checkbox-style states. Individual removal, Clear all, Clear search and Search another brand let people adjust selections or start another query without losing their draft.
- Phones up to 600px show three initial results; wider screens show five. See more reveals another three or five from the returned matches, without nested result scrolling. The existing search API still returns at most 20 matches.
- Nomination save/continue and organizer experiment-creation actions use forest green #123C2E with ivory #F5F0E8. Selected chips use the original pale #F7F9EF with #C3CEAE borders. The sand concept was rejected and is not part of this release.
- Manual names remain available after search resolves or fails and are marked For review. Catalog IDs and images, already-on-board navigation, the 50-nomination limit, saved selection restoration and save-before-board continuation are retained. Enter acts on the highlighted result; it does not silently create a manual nomination during loading.
