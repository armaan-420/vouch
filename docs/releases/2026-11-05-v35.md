### Approved board behavior

- A small pale chip beneath each rated score displays the total number of active votes: Yes + Maybe + Pass. It uses “1 vote” or “N votes,” compact notation for large totals, 9px type and a 16px visual height with 2px × 5px padding. The background is #e9ecdf and text #566044. Unrated products show no vote chip.
- Clicking the chip opens only “The community says,” the exact total number of votes, and the colored Yes / Maybe / Pass counts. Repeated product-name text, formula explanations and footnotes are removed from this popover. Click outside or Escape dismisses it; only one breakdown is open at once.
- The shared component applies to collection boards, community-nominated products and the overall board. The overall board retains the latest active verdict per anonymous browser and canonical SKU across included collections.
- Yes remains an item vouch, Maybe a Soft-Vouch for brand trust, and Pass a pass. The chip counts each as one raw vote, while scoring still weights item vouches and passes at 1 and brand trust at 0.5. Skips and invalid verdicts are excluded. No vote reset or data migration is required.
- Version 34 shipped a larger pale item-vouch chip. The later size, total-vote and content revisions were reviewed locally and approved before version 35 publication. The rejected dark arrow/count control is superseded by this design. Historical entries and source artifacts remain preserved.
