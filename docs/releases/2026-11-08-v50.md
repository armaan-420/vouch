### Research and publication checks

- New research targets ten distinct brands and requests actual TikTok content, at least one Wirecutter brand (two preferred), another brand recommended by the first organic Google result, and an AI recommendation corroborated by reputable editorial/manufacturer sources.
- Evidence is private and tied to exact brand/model records. Automatically accepted non-AI evidence must appear in returned provider sources; Google also needs the actual Google search page and rank 1. Organizer-checked evidence carries a marker and timestamp. URL provenance is not proof that a claim is correct.
- All four channel checks and a valid photo for every product remaining in the final lineup are required at approval, including legacy drafts and organizer additions. Missing or inaccessible evidence keeps the draft private. Existing live categories are not retroactively unpublished.
- Invalid channel evidence becomes a source-review warning while valid product research can continue. Malformed output, invalid products and unsafe pick source URLs still fail validation. Research limits are now 16,000 output tokens and 16 tool calls; the daily cap remains 20 starts, not a dollar budget.
- Recover saved research resumes an existing saved response without a new model start. Refresh research requests a new budgeted attempt. Classified failures and authorized diagnostics distinguish incomplete responses, HTTP failures and validation failures without raw provider errors or secrets.

### Organizer workflows

- Suggestions open a visual review with Product lineup, Research & sources, and Details & history. Photo cards, model-name differences, missing requirements, expandable evidence and source warnings make launch readiness visible. The selected tab and expanded sections survive refresh. List rows have a Review link; less frequent actions move to More actions.
- Find missing photos retries current-category search, broader catalog search and saved product-URL lookup. A photo match requires the normalized brand and every model word; it cannot substitute a different product. A request checks up to ten missing items, three at a time, with per-item outcomes and a loading state. Add photo focuses the existing manual URL field.
- Provider identity is retained with research names/order. Organizer photo overrides survive storage and hydration. Cached search hits refresh photo snapshots; photographed snapshots survive photo-less/circular duplicate aliases through bounded traversal.
- Experiment detail opens Products, Settings and Participation, Missing Products, and Community Additions. Products show experiment-specific Vouch/Soft-Vouch/Pass counts, total and score without visitor identifiers. Reorder/remove/add/photo changes use lineup revisions to reject stale edits; removal retains history and re-adding the same ID retains votes. One product per brand and the 32-product cap remain.
- Disable hides the public page/APIs and overall-board participation while retaining data. Reactivate requires photos and preserves the existing schedule. Permanent Delete requires typing the path, removes experiment-specific activity and associated suggestion, and reserves the deleted path. Shared catalog records and personal account opinions remain. Cleanup is recoverable across records: failed cleanup leaves a disabled row for retry. This is distinct from recoverable suggestion deletion.

### Admin access

The hosted organizer now uses a four-digit server-configured PIN in place of the former ChatGPT/email allowlist flow. Successful entry creates a fixed 24-hour server session with a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookie; only the random token hash is stored. Lock admin revokes that session. PIN changes invalidate existing sessions. Five unsuccessful attempts per IP-derived bucket within 15 minutes block further attempts until the window expires. Admin writes require same-origin requests. Participant email-code accounts remain separate. No PIN value or other credential is included here.
