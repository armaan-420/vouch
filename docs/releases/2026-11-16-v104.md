## 2026-11-16 — Version 104: easier product tags and a clearer Board

Published on <date start="2026-11-16" /> (America/New_York). [Open Vouch](https://v4ch.app/sunscreen?board=1).

### New in this release

- **Tag products directly from The Board.** Every product now shows a compact, always-visible row of tappable tags beneath its image and name. The standard set is **Design, Quality, Staple, Value**. Counts appear when a tag has votes, and a dark chip shows your own selection. Tap again to remove it. Changes appear immediately and are undone if saving fails. The strip scrolls sideways on small screens.
- **Scores are easier to read and open.** The score is larger, with its vote count tucked directly underneath. Clicking either opens the same “The community says” popup. The three verdict tiles use single-line labels, no emojis, and balanced space above the labels and below the numbers.
- **See some of the people behind an item vouch.** The popup can show up to four public vouchers, with your own face first when you are one of them. Brand-only trust and negative responses stay anonymous counts. Hidden names use an unlinked question mark. The number of public vouchers can differ from the Board's anonymous vote totals.
- **“See all” opens the right product page.** It takes you to that item's “People who vouch” section, keeping the collection context so you can return to its Board. Named initials link to the person's public profile.
- **A product-led sign-in screen.** The email sign-in/signup screens now include a Beauty of Joseon product illustration. Its 8.8 score is explicitly marked as a sample, not a live rating.

The “+” beside the tags is present, but adding a new custom tag is not available to visitors yet. It shows a brief explanation rather than creating a tag.

### Catch-up since the last Notion release entry

The previous documented checkpoint was version 50. This entry also brings the notes up to date for the source changes included in the current release:

- **Browse more of Vouch.** Explore lists open categories with cover images, category names and direct voting/Board links. Boards suggest other categories after the rankings. The shared header now uses Explore, Leaderboard and Products; on phones it sits in the menu. The header hides while scrolling down and returns while scrolling up.
- **Find products and keep your collection.** Product pages bring together scores, public item vouchers, tags, image galleries and available merchant links. Products search supports names, recorded tags and a limited set of everyday phrases. Personal-fit estimates use related saved item ratings and are labeled estimates; this is not general AI search or a prediction of satisfaction. Mobile nominations use a focused search dialog with one-tap saving.
- **A simpler account journey.** Email codes serve both sign-in and signup. Participants can save their collection and manage their profile. Verified exact-domain Wharton inboxes are distinguished from the historical early-tester badge. The Wharton contributor leaderboard recognizes unique current public item vouches from eligible verified members, honors opt-outs, and excludes hidden/private profiles. Verification proves inbox control, not current enrollment or product expertise.
- **Four tags across the product journey.** The experiment card, product page and collection share saved tag selections. Signed-in selections follow the account across devices. Ease of use and Reliability have been retired from public chips while their historical data remains stored.
- **Updated score weighting.** Since the previous checkpoint, an item Vouch contributes 10, Brand vouch 7 and Nope 0, with one neutral prior of 5. Skips do not count. Existing stored answers are read through this formula; this Board layout release makes no further scoring change.
- **Better organizer tools.** Organizers can edit category covers and display names without changing the page address or votes, review people/accounts, send verification, change or add an email, disable/re-enable access and revoke sessions. Product lookup batches saved catalog writes and avoids unnecessary live lookups during routine reads; saved-photo recovery has clearer outcomes.
- **Resumable category research.** Research is a saved private workspace with independent source tasks, exact-model checks, evidence/access notes, pause/resume, source repairs and organizer-controlled lineup changes. Missing source breadth is advisory; publishing still requires at least five distinct brands, photos and explicit organizer approval. Reddit is optional anecdotal evidence. A configured hourly Sites recovery schedule and a GitHub fallback support saved jobs; this release does not claim a fresh unattended run or source-quality benchmark. Daily private-category tools enforce the existing three-slot Eastern-day cap.
- **GitHub and hosting stay separate.** Main-branch syncing and automated tests are in place. Sites deployment still publishes a checked saved version separately from a GitHub push.
