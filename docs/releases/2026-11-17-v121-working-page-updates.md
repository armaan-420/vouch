## Product definition

[Notion page](https://app.notion.com/p/40ad2a3caa8a8160afd6e082d848f7f7)

Use the header for site-wide search and Find for the full results. Products is the filterable library and can include products with public Vouches outside Edits. Product pages support photo-area verdicts, public recommendations, retailer comparison and related picks. Wharton has a dedicated entry and category flow. Public profiles show top categories.

## Visual style

[Notion page](https://app.notion.com/p/40ad2a3caa8a813f823ed60828358479)

On phones, the shared header puts Search, Profile and Menu together. Board rows can place public vouchers beside product imagery. Product cards center category labels above the voucher row and place eligible quick rating controls beside the image on phones. Product detail puts verdict controls on the photo and uses a compact expandable retailer comparison.
