## November 17, 2026 — Vouch versions 108–121

### Finding and sharing products

- **One search across Vouch.** Search from the page header to see Vouch results first and broader catalog matches as they arrive. Open the full results on Find. Products remains a browsable library with category and community filters. The first mobile release added a floating Vouch shortcut. The later header puts Search, Profile and Menu together and uses the existing in-page nomination action.
- **Vouch for products outside an Edit.** Give a verdict from the photo area on a product page. Your choice collapses into a compact control you can reopen to change it. New visitors can sign in and finish the action they started. Eligible products can be nominated to a matching Edit, with a choice when more than one fits and a retry if adding to the Edit fails after the profile save. Products with a qualifying public Vouch can appear in the library even if they have never been in an Edit.
- **More context on the Board and product pages.** Board rows can show named public endorsers next to the item. Rating flows support a short optional inline note, and product pages show public recommendation comments below the people section. Hidden identities stay hidden. Phone layouts use a compact rating state while typing or toggling tags.
- **A Wharton entry page.** The Wharton experiments have their own landing page and email verification flow. Returning verified participants can go straight to categories. Boards show category switches and participation prompts based on the participant’s saved activity. Lip balm is available and Carry-on luggage is shown as upcoming.

### Profiles and shopping

- **See what someone recommends most.** Public profiles show top categories in the header, with a control to reveal more when available. Product cards in the library and collections place category labels above a consistent voucher row. Eligible mobile cards put quick rating controls beside the image.
- **Compare retailers on the product page.** The purchase card starts with the available direct link. Open the comparison to request offers. When prices are available, it shows up to five offers and marks the lowest displayed price. Vouch discloses that it may earn a commission. Prices and availability are set by retailers.
- **Find other picks in the same category.** Product pages can show up to three rated alternatives when there are at least two. Newly added products invite the first vote and show ranked alternatives when available.

### Organizer tools and reliability

- **Manage categories in one place.** Organizers can create or rename categories, assign a parent and add aliases that help match existing labels and provider products. Category IDs stay stable. Unmatched Edit labels are flagged for review, and the public Products filter still lists categories in a flat menu.
- **Keep a research draft moving when sources fall short.** Individual source gaps become review notes. Up to three independent checks can advance together. Dedicated Wirecutter and manufacturer tasks were retired, with completed evidence retained. Editorial search can still find Wirecutter. Category approval still requires five distinct brands, a photo for each selected product and an organizer’s approval.
- **Reduce repeated loading work.** Vouch overlaps independent inventory and catalog reads and reuses repeated account and settings reads within a single request. The controlled tests measured specific operations using simulated delays. Live page speed remains unmeasured.
