## November 18, 2026 — Vouch versions 122–138

### Wharton home and pilot

Eligible new Wharton profiles get a skippable three-step walkthrough. A one-time Board question asks verified participants whether seeing who vouched would change what they would buy. The Wharton leaderboard’s Who to ask section surfaces classmates’ public recommendations and links to their picks.

- Verified Wharton members now use `/wharton` as their return home from headers, product pages, profiles, search, the leaderboard, and Boards. Browser-only, unverified, pending-verification, non-Wharton, and signed-out visitors retain the existing routes.
- Returning members see progress and clear Start, Continue, and Vouched card states. Continue resumes at the first unanswered product.
- Completing every live Wharton category unlocks the other open categories. Unlocking is verified and saved by the server, is idempotent, and can keep a per-account browser fallback during a network failure.
- The Board presents Unlock all categories only for eligible members who still have additional open categories. The existing actions remain otherwise.
- The organizer pilot report and CSV now include funnel, completion depth, return, trust, pulse, sharing, unlock, and post-unlock-start metrics.

### Sharing and product experience

- Public individual Vouches, Wharton picks, category winners, and the Wharton leaderboard can be shared as story cards. Individual Vouch cards can include a retailer link when available.
- Product pages place Share above the image and clarify the direct retailer action. The Guava lip-balm record has its direct retailer destination.
- Board people rows share a consistent layout. Profile return navigation and account/catalog photo fallbacks reduce broken context and imagery.

### Organizer visibility

- Product Inventory can include qualifying public product-page recommendations separately from Edit votes.
