## November 19, 2026 — Vouch versions 139–152

### One next step on the Board

Version 152 replaces the experiment Board’s two next-step cards with a compact dock. Wharton members can resume the current category, rate the next one, unlock additional categories when eligible, or invite a classmate. Everyday visitors can start vouching or invite a friend. On phones the dock appears after the first scroll, then stays available. More opens as a phone sheet or desktop popover and contains the other available actions, including category suggestions and the leaderboard. The Board leaves room for its footer, and the missing-favorite action follows the last ranking.

Wharton invites include a referral when the member’s handle matches the supported format. Invites use device sharing, clipboard, or a selectable link. Pilot impressions fire once per Board render on first reveal. Pilot actions now include Invite and More, and sharing picks retains its existing attribution. Everyday Boards emit no new pilot events. Draft Boards omit the dock, and the overall Board retains its original end card.

### Other changes since version 138

- **Wharton demo and progress (139–140, 150).** The resettable demo supports trying the pilot without recording live votes. The demo Board shows category progress. Version 150 restored the interim Wharton continuation card and invite, which the new dock replaces.
- **Organizer workspace (141).** Navigation, account management, inventory, suggestions, feedback, and pilot reporting were reorganized into a more consistent organizer interface.
- **Loading, sign-in and search (142–145, 147–149).** Page loading and avatar presentation were stabilized, the product-page skeleton was restored, the homepage sign-in action was aligned, and shared navigation stays hidden until sign-in is resolved where applicable. The guide v1.2 pass updated search and related UI. Catalog search retained its looping loader with reduced-motion handling, and header and nomination search got an older Safari compatibility fix.
- **Product-page Vouch notes (146).** Signed-in contributors can create, edit, and remove a note on a product they vouch for. Public display follows the opinion’s visibility and profile name settings.
- **Profile achievements (151).** Eligible Wharton profiles display earned category-expert and current leaderboard Top 5 achievements where applicable.
