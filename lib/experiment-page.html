ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

A tiny test of good taste.

Scores balance community approval with the number of verdicts, on a 0–10 scale. Two vouches and no passes earn 9.0; ten earn 9.8. A 10.0 requires at least 100 Vouch or Pass verdicts and near-unanimous support. Haven’t tried is excluded; products without verdicts are Unrated. Anonymous browser visits and flow progress are saved to the shared database. No name or email is requested to participate.

Search results come from live brand catalogs and refresh periodically. Selecting a product saves its exact identity and photo. Two distinct browsers selecting the same sunscreen add it to the community board automatically. Name-only nominations are looked up in the organizer inbox.

Each browser has one active vote per product and can nominate multiple missing products. Clearing browser cookies or using another browser creates a new anonymous participant.
