ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

A tiny test of good taste.

Scores balance weighted community approval with the amount of feedback, on a 0–10 scale. Item vouches and passes carry full weight; brand vouches carry half weight and apply only to the item shown. Two item vouches and no passes earn 9.0; two brand vouches earn 8.3. A 10.0 requires at least 100 weighted verdicts and near-unanimous support. Haven’t tried is excluded; products without verdicts are Unrated. Anonymous browser visits and flow progress are saved to the shared database. No name or email is requested to participate.

Search covers connected brand and retailer catalogs and the worldwide sunscreen category in Open Beauty Facts. Catalogs refresh periodically. Coverage is not exhaustive. Selecting a product saves its exact identity and photo. Two distinct browsers selecting the same sunscreen add it to the community board automatically. Name-only nominations are looked up in the organizer inbox.

Each browser has one active vote per product and can nominate multiple missing products. Clearing browser cookies or using another browser creates a new anonymous participant.
