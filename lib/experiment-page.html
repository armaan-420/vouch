ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

A tiny test of good taste.

Scores average community responses on a 0–10 scale: Vouch is worth 10, Soft-Vouch is worth 7, and Pass is worth 0. One neutral response worth 5 tempers scores with little feedback. Two Vouches earn 8.3; two Soft-Vouches earn 6.3. Brand trust applies only to the item shown. Soft-Vouches alone approach 7; higher scores require item Vouches. Haven’t tried is excluded; products without verdicts are Unrated. Anonymous browser visits and flow progress are saved to the shared database. After voting, you can optionally add your name and email. Your name appears alongside your item vouches by default; control name visibility in your profile. The email entered on that form is saved privately to your profile so you can sign in later with an email verification code. A Wharton address also earns a Wharton early tester badge. This badge is self-reported and does not verify affiliation. You can return to your profile from another browser by verifying the email you supplied. Email sign-in uses a verification code to restore your collection across devices. Your email address stays private.

Search covers connected brand and retailer catalogs and the worldwide sunscreen category in Open Beauty Facts. Catalogs refresh periodically. Live search, when enabled, uses Channel3 and Shopify Catalog. Coverage is not exhaustive. Selecting a live product saves its identity; its photo comes from the provider when shown. Two distinct browsers selecting the same sunscreen add it to the community board automatically. Name-only nominations are looked up in the organizer inbox.

Contains data from Open Beauty Facts under the Open Database License. Open Beauty Facts photos: CC BY-SA 3.0. Also includes brand and retailer catalogs. Download database extract. Coverage varies; missing products can be added by name.

Each browser has one active vote per product and can nominate multiple missing products. Clearing browser cookies or using another browser creates a new anonymous participant.
