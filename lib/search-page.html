How search & personal fit work

Search matches product names, brands, categories and recorded product tags. It understands common phrases such as “doesn’t leak” and “isn’t greasy.” A product only matches a requested feature when that feature is recorded. Coverage grows as we add products and details.

Personal fit is an early estimate from the features shared with at least two products you’ve vouched for or passed on in the same category. A neutral starting point tempers small samples. It is not a prediction of satisfaction. Your own verdict replaces an estimate on products you’ve already rated; brand trust does not count as an item endorsement.

Community scores use Vouches (10), Soft-Vouches (7) and Passes (0), with one neutral response (5). Search combines the latest response per browser across public collections, so its score can differ from an individual board. Ratings are not verified purchases. Prices, availability and unrecorded features cannot yet be filtered reliably.
