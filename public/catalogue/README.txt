Product photos: CC BY-SA 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
The extract selects named sunscreen records from every page of the worldwide sunscreen category, omits bundles, and normalizes the product fields for Vouch search. Brand and retailer catalogs are independent sources and are not included in this extract.
